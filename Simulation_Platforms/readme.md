# ✅ 1. Agent-Based Simulation Platforms (Classical MAS)

## **NetLogo**

* **Why choose it:**
  Easiest MAS platform for beginners; excellent for rapid prototyping of agent behavior; huge library of ready-to-run models; great for social science, ecology, economics.
* **Site:**
  [NetLogo Official Site](https://ccl.northwestern.edu/netlogo/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

---

## **Repast (Recursive Porous Agent Simulation Toolkit)**

* **Why choose it:**
  Very mature research platform; Java-based; supports both discrete-event and time-stepped simulations; well-suited for large-scale complex MAS.
* **Site:**
  [Repast Suite](https://repast.github.io/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

---

## **MASON (Multi-Agent Simulator of Networks)**

* **Why choose it:**
  Fast, lightweight, Java-based; excellent for high-performance MAS and custom algorithms; widely used in academic research.
* **Site:**
  [MASON MAS Simulator](https://cs.gmu.edu/~eclab/projects/mason/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

---

## **GAMA Platform**

* **Why choose it:**
  Highly expressive modeling language; strong visualization; great for GIS-based and spatial simulations; widely used in urban systems, environment, and epidemiology.
* **Site:**
  [GAMA Platform](https://gama-platform.org/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
